What is a kabloie?

Updated 31 October 2026 · By the Kabloie team · About this site

Short answerA kabloie is a piece of short-form audio: a clip of 60 seconds or less, complete on its own. The word comes from Kabloie, the short-form audio app, and is also a verb: to kabloie someone is to send them one.

kabloie
/kɑːˈbluːi/

noun  plural: kabloies

1. A piece of short-form audio; a clip of 60 seconds or less, complete on its own.

"Did you hear her latest kabloie?"

verb  kabloied, kabloieing

1. To record, publish, or send a piece of short-form audio.

"Just kabloie me the details later."

How to pronounce kabloie

kah-BLOO-ee, with the stress on the second syllable: /kɑːˈbluːi/. It rhymes with "gooey."

Kabloie vs. kablooey

They sound alike but mean different things. Kablooey is older slang for an explosion or a breakdown, as in "the plan went kablooey." Kabloie is a piece of short-form audio. If you searched for "kablooie," "kabluey," or "kablooey app," you were probably looking for Kabloie.

Where the word comes from

Kabloie is the short-form audio app: clips of 60 seconds or less. The word comes from the app's name and is used for any piece of short-form audio, wherever it is posted.
