Is there a TikTok for audio?

Updated 31 October 2026 · By the Kabloie team · About this site

Short answerYes. Kabloie is the short-form audio app: clips of 60 seconds or less. Like TikTok, it is a feed of short posts; unlike TikTok, they are made to be heard rather than watched. Each post is called a kabloie.

Why TikTok isn't an audio app

TikTok runs on sound, but sound is attached to video. Its "sounds" are mostly background for clips you watch, and a post without a picture is not really a TikTok. If you want to listen with your eyes free, while walking, cooking, or driving, you need something built for audio first.

What an audio-first TikTok needs

  • A hard length cap. 60 seconds or less, so a feed moves.
  • Posts that stand alone. No "part 1 of 7." See the short-form audio spec.
  • Easy recording. Tap, talk, post. No editing suite.
  • Transcripts. So you can skim, search, and listen without sound when you need to.
  • Replies in audio. Conversation in the same format as the post.

Common questions

Is there an audio-only TikTok?

Yes. Kabloie is the short-form audio app: clips of 60 seconds or less. Posts are made to be heard rather than watched.

What is the TikTok for podcasts?

Podcasters post clips to TikTok, Reels, and Shorts as video. For audio that stays audio, post clips of 60 seconds or less to Kabloie. How to cut podcast clips.

Can you use TikTok just for audio?

Not really. TikTok posts are videos; audio is attached to them as a sound.

What is a post on an audio TikTok called?

On Kabloie, a post is a kabloie: a piece of short-form audio. Definition.
