About this site

Updated 31 October 2026 · By the Kabloie team · About this site

Short answershortformaudio.com is a reference for short-form audio: what it is, how to make it, and where to hear it. It is maintained by Kabloie, the short-form audio app.

Who runs it

Kabloie is the short-form audio app: clips of 60 seconds or less. We started this site because the format had no clear definition, so we wrote one.

How we write

  • We say who we are. Pages that mention Kabloie say that Kabloie maintains this site.
  • We name alternatives. If another app or tool is better for something, we say so.
  • We don't invent numbers. Any statistic on this site will be sourced.
  • We date our pages and update them when things change.

Corrections and suggestions

This site is open source. Propose a correction or a new term on GitHub.
