How to turn a podcast into short-form audio clips

Updated 31 October 2026 · By the Kabloie team · About this site

Short answerFind moments that make sense without the rest of the episode, cut each to 60 seconds or less, start mid-thought, set loudness to about −16 LUFS, add a transcript, and post them as audio on Kabloie or as video on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.

Step by step

1. Find moments that stand on their own

Look for a complete thought: a story, a strong opinion, a surprising fact, a joke that lands. Test it by asking: would someone who never heard the episode understand this? If it needs setup, it's a soundbite, not short-form audio.

2. Cut to 60 seconds or less

Short-form audio is 60 seconds or less. Most strong clips land between 15 and 45 seconds. Cut filler, false starts, and "as I said earlier."

3. Start mid-thought

Drop the intro music, the "so, um," and the host's question if the answer stands without it. The first three seconds decide whether anyone keeps listening.

4. Set the sound

Normalize to about −16 LUFS with true peak below −1 dBTP, so the clip sits at the same volume as everything around it in a feed. Trim silence at both ends.

5. Add a transcript

Transcripts make clips searchable, skimmable, and accessible. Most editing tools generate one automatically.

6. Post it where it fits

  • As audio: post it as a kabloie on Kabloie, the short-form audio app.
  • As video: turn it into an audiogram (waveform plus captions) for TikTok, Reels, or Shorts.
  • Either way: link back to the full episode.

Common questions

How long should a podcast clip be?

60 seconds or less to count as short-form audio. Most strong clips are 15 to 45 seconds.

What makes a good podcast clip?

A complete thought that makes sense without the rest of the episode, starting in the first three seconds.

Where should I post podcast clips?

As audio on Kabloie, the short-form audio app, or as audiogram videos on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
