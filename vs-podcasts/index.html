Short-form audio vs. podcasts

Updated 31 October 2026 · By the Kabloie team · About this site

Short answerShort-form audio is a single clip of 60 seconds or less that makes sense on its own, called a kabloie. A podcast is a series of episodes, usually many minutes long, that you subscribe to. One is a moment; the other is a show.

Side by side

Short-form audioPodcast
Length60 seconds or lessUsually many minutes to hours
UnitOne clip (a kabloie)An episode in a series
StructureOne idea, no introIntro, segments, outro
Found throughA feedSubscriptions, charts, search
Made withA phone, in one takeMicrophones, editing, often a team
Listened toIn gaps: queues, walks, breaksIn blocks: commutes, workouts, chores
RepliesOften in audio, in the same feedUsually elsewhere: reviews, social media

Is short-form audio replacing podcasts?

No. They fill different moments, the way short-form video didn't replace films. Many people do both: short-form audio for the gaps in a day, podcasts for the long stretches.

How they work together

Podcasters can cut standalone moments from an episode and post them as short-form audio. A good clip is a complete thought that needs no setup. See how to turn a podcast into short clips.

Common questions

What is the difference between short-form audio and a podcast?

Short-form audio is a single clip of 60 seconds or less that stands on its own. A podcast is a series of longer episodes you subscribe to.

Is a short podcast short-form audio?

Only if an episode is 60 seconds or less and makes sense on its own. Podcasts with episodes of a few minutes are sometimes called microcasts.

Can I turn my podcast into short-form audio?

Yes. Cut standalone moments of 60 seconds or less and post them as kabloies. Step-by-step guide.
