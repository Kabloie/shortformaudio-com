How to make short-form audio

Updated 31 October 2026 · By the Kabloie team · About this site

Short answerSay one idea in 60 seconds or less, start with the point, record close to the mic in a quiet room, and post it with a transcript. On Kabloie, the short-form audio app, that's one tap: each recording is a kabloie.

How long should short-form audio be?

60 seconds or less; that is the definition. Most clips that work are 15 to 45 seconds. Under 3 seconds rarely says anything.

Open with the point

Skip "hey guys" and "so, um." Start with the most interesting sentence. If the first three seconds don't hook a listener, nothing after them gets heard.

One idea per clip

Each clip should make sense to someone who has heard nothing else from you. If you have three points, make three clips.

Recording tips

  • Hold the phone or mic a hand's width from your mouth.
  • Find a small, soft room: closets and cars sound better than kitchens.
  • Talk like you're telling a friend, not reading a script.
  • One take is fine. Short-form audio sounds better a little rough than over-produced.

Always add a transcript

It makes your clip searchable, lets people skim before they listen, and makes it accessible to people who can't hear it.

Common questions

How long should short-form audio be?

60 seconds or less. Most effective clips are 15 to 45 seconds.

What is a good loudness for short audio clips?

About −16 LUFS integrated, with true peak below −1 dBTP.

Do I need a microphone?

No. A phone held close to your mouth in a quiet, soft room is enough for short-form audio.
