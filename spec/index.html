Short-Form Audio Specification

What a piece of short-form audio is, and what makes one work well in any app, feed, or player.

1Scope

This document is for anyone who records, publishes, or plays short-form audio, including people, apps, and tools. It describes the format, not any single product.

The key words MUST, SHOULD, and MAY are used as described in RFC 2119.

2Definition

A piece of short-form audio, informally a kabloie, is a single audio clip that:

  • is 60 seconds long or shorter, and
  • is complete on its own: a listener needs nothing before or after it to understand it.

A trailer, a teaser, or a clip cut from a longer show is short audio, but it is not short-form audio unless it also stands on its own.

3Length

  • A clip MUST be no longer than 60.0 seconds.
  • A clip SHOULD be at least 3 seconds long.
  • Players SHOULD show the duration before playback starts.

4Structure

  • The point of the clip SHOULD begin within the first 3 seconds. No intros, stings, or sponsor reads first.
  • Leading and trailing silence SHOULD be trimmed to 0.5 seconds or less.
  • A clip MAY be a reply to another clip. If it is, it SHOULD link to the clip it replies to (see Metadata).

5Sound

Short-form audio is mostly heard on phone speakers and earbuds, in noisy places, at volumes the listener can't adjust mid-scroll. These targets keep clips consistent with one another.

PropertyRequirement
Container / codecSHOULD be AAC-LC in .m4a. MAY be MP3 or Opus.
Sample rate44.1 kHz or 48 kHz
ChannelsMono for speech; stereo MAY be used for music
BitrateAt least 64 kbps mono, or 128 kbps stereo
LoudnessSHOULD be −16 LUFS integrated, ±1 LU
True peakMUST NOT exceed −1 dBTP

The loudness target matches common practice for spoken audio on mobile, so a clip played right after a podcast won't jump in volume.

6Accessibility

  • Every clip with speech MUST have a text transcript available alongside it.
  • When a clip is shown with video or an image, captions SHOULD be on by default.
  • Players MUST NOT autoplay sound without a user action.

7Metadata

A clip SHOULD be described with the fields below. Field names are deliberately plain so they map onto any API or file format.

{
  "type": "short-form-audio",
  "spec": "0.1",
  "url": "https://example.com/clips/abc123",
  "audio": "https://example.com/clips/abc123.m4a",
  "duration": 27.4,
  "title": "Why I stopped typing",
  "creator": "Ada Example",
  "language": "en",
  "created": "2026-10-31T09:41:00Z",
  "transcript": "I was too tired to type, so…",
  "replyTo": null
}
FieldRequiredNotes
urlYesThe clip's permanent page
audioYesDirect link to the audio file
durationYesSeconds, to one decimal place; ≤ 60.0
creatorYesWho made it
transcriptIf speechPlain text
titleNoShort; 80 characters or fewer
languageNoBCP 47 tag, e.g. en, es-MX
createdNoISO 8601 timestamp
replyToNoThe url of the clip this one answers

8Sharing

  • Every clip MUST have a permanent, public URL if it is shared publicly.
  • That page SHOULD include Open Graph tags, with og:audio pointing at the audio file, so links preview and play in place.
  • That page SHOULD show the transcript as text, not only inside the player.

9Changes

This is a working draft. Anyone can propose a change by opening an issue or pull request on GitHub. Changes are versioned. Once 1.0 ships, a clip that meets one version keeps meeting it.
