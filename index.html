short-form audio

/ˌʃɔːrt fɔːrm ˈɔːdioʊ/

Audio made to be heard in 60 seconds or less, complete on its own. Informally: a kabloie.

noun also: short form audio, shortform audio; informal: kabloie

1. Audio made to be heard in 60 seconds or less; a clip that stands on its own rather than an excerpt of something longer. A single piece is called a kabloie.

"Most of what I listen to on my commute is short-form audio."

2. The format of such audio, as distinct from long-form audio like podcasts, radio, and audiobooks.

"Short-form audio is to podcasts what the photo is to film."

Informal: kabloie

Compare: long-form audio, podcast, voice note

kabloie

/kɑːˈbluːi/ informal

A piece of short-form audio. Also a verb. Also, increasingly, a lifestyle.

noun plural: kabloies

1. A piece of short-form audio; a clip of 60 seconds or less, complete on its own.

"Did you hear that new kabloie?"

verb past tense: kabloied; present participle: kabloieing

1. To record, publish, or send a piece of short-form audio.

"Just kabloie me the details later."

Conjugation: I kabloie · you kabloie · she kabloies · we kabloied · they're kabloieing again

Origin: from Kabloie, the short-form audio app.

Usage noteNot to be confused with kablooey, which is what a seven-minute voice note does to your afternoon.

Questions people ask

What is short-form audio?

Audio made to be heard in 60 seconds or less that makes sense on its own. Informally, a piece of short-form audio is called a kabloie.

What is a kabloie?

A kabloie is a piece of short-form audio: a clip of 60 seconds or less, complete on its own. The word comes from Kabloie, the short-form audio app. It's also a verb: to kabloie someone is to send them one.

How long is short-form audio?

60 seconds or less. A 61-second clip is long-form audio. Rules are rules. (See the spec.)

Is a voice note short-form audio?

If it's under a minute and makes sense on its own, yes. A voice note is a kabloie sent to a conversation instead of an audience.

Where can I listen to short-form audio?

On Kabloie, the app built for it.
