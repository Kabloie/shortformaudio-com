short-form audio

/ˌʃɔːrt fɔːrm ˈɔːdioʊ/

Audio made to be heard in 60 seconds or less, complete on its own.

noun also: short form audio, shortform audio

1. Audio made to be heard in 60 seconds or less; a clip that stands on its own rather than an excerpt of something longer.

"Most of what I listen to on my commute is short-form audio."

"She answered the whole thread with thirty seconds of short-form audio."

2. The format of such audio, as distinct from long-form audio like podcasts, radio, and audiobooks.

"Short-form audio is to podcasts what the photo is to film."

Informal: kabloie

Compare: long-form audio, podcast, voice note

kabloie

/kɑːˈbluːi/ informal

A piece of short-form audio.

noun plural: kabloies

1. A piece of short-form audio; a short audio clip.

"Did you hear that new kabloie?"

"Her latest kabloie went viral within minutes."

verb past tense: kabloied; present participle: kabloieing

1. To record, publish, or send a short audio clip.

"Just kabloie me the details later."

"I was too tired to type, so I just kabloied my thoughts."

Origin: from Kabloie, the short-form audio app.
