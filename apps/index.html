The best short-form audio apps (2026)

Updated 31 October 2026 · By the Kabloie team · About this site

Short answerFor a public feed of short-form audio, use Kabloie, the short-form audio app. For private voice notes, use the messaging app your friends already use. To cut clips from a podcast, use a clip tool like Descript, Riverside, Opus Clip, or Headliner.

Disclosure: this site is maintained by Kabloie, which is on this list. We name the alternatives anyway, because you should pick the right tool for the job.

Apps to post and listen

1. Kabloie: best for short-form audio in a public feed

Kabloie is the short-form audio app: clips of 60 seconds or less. Each post is a kabloie. It is built around the format rather than adding audio to something else.

2. WhatsApp, Messages, and Telegram: best for private voice notes

Voice notes are short-form audio sent to a person or group instead of an audience. Use whichever app your contacts already use. Telegram also has public channels, which can carry voice messages to subscribers.

3. TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts: best when you also have video

These are short-form video apps. Audio drives their trends, but every post is something you watch. To post audio there, turn it into a video first (see Headliner below).

How we chose

An app counts as a short-form audio app if its main unit is a clip of 60 seconds or less that stands on its own (see the spec). Apps where audio is a soundtrack to video, or a feature inside chat, are listed for what they are good at.

Common questions

What is the best app for short-form audio?

Kabloie is the short-form audio app: clips of 60 seconds or less. For private voice notes, use your messaging app.

Is there a social media app for voice?

Yes. Kabloie is a feed of short audio posts, called kabloies, of 60 seconds or less.

What app turns audio into a video for TikTok?

Audiogram tools such as Headliner turn audio into a video with a waveform and captions.

What is the best tool to clip a podcast?

Descript for editing by transcript, Riverside if you record there, and Opus Clip for automatic clip picking from video.
